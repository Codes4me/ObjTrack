How it works: clicking a point samples its color and marks it as the tracked point. In video mode, pressing Play (or Step Forward/Back) searches a window around the last known position each frame for pixels matching that color and logs the centroid automatically. A manual click automatically advances one frame afterward, so you can click through an object frame-by-frame. Click again at any time to correct the point or re-acquire a lost target — this recalibrates the tracked color. Works best when the object has a color that stands out from its background.
